What are macros? Macronutrients are the three main nutrients your body uses for fuel. Protein builds and repairs muscle, supports hormones, and keeps you full. Carbohydrates are your body and brain's preferred energy source. Fat supports hormone production, brain function, and nutrient absorption. The percentages below tell you how much of your daily calories should come from each one.
Hormone Support Split 35% Protein  ·  40% Carbs  ·  25% Fat

Designed for women in perimenopause and menopause. Higher protein preserves muscle as estrogen declines. Moderate carbs support energy and mood. Healthy fats support hormone production. This is Leah's recommended default for 40+ women.

Higher Protein Split 40% Protein  ·  35% Carbs  ·  25% Fat

For women who strength train heavily or want to prioritize muscle building and fat loss simultaneously. Higher protein keeps you fuller longer and supports muscle repair after intense training sessions.

Carb-Forward Split 30% Protein  ·  45% Carbs  ·  25% Fat

For women who feel and perform better with more carbohydrates — endurance athletes, women who experience low energy on lower-carb approaches, or those who find carb restriction triggers bingeing or restriction cycles. If your body runs better on carbs, trust that.

Resting Energy Expenditure (REE)
—
calories per day at complete rest
This is the minimum your body needs to survive — breathing, circulation, cell repair, and hormone production. Never eat below this number. Eating under your REE signals starvation to your body, suppresses your metabolism, and disrupts hormone production.
